03 / THE MECHANISM

What's actually happening?

Three steps. One continuous flow of data.

01
↘

Download

The network delivers compressed video data. Its speed determines how quickly new video can arrive.

02

Buffer

The player temporarily stores incoming data as playable video, up to its buffer capacity.

03
▶

Play

Playback consumes the stored video continuously. If the reserve reaches zero, playback stalls.

THE SIMPLE MODEL

Buffer change rate

Download speed / Video bitrate − 1

A positive result means the buffer grows during playback. Zero means it stays roughly level. A negative result means it drains.

20 / 5 = 4× · Buffer grows
5 / 5 = 1× · Buffer stays level
2 / 5 = 0.4× · Buffer drains

Model assumption: This is a simplified constant-bitrate model. Real streaming uses chunks, adaptive bitrate selection, variable-bitrate encoding, protocol overhead and player-specific buffering rules. The simulation expresses the buffer in seconds of playable video; incoming data is converted using download speed ÷ video bitrate.
